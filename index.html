Aquatic Disease Radar: log tilapia mortality, see where it clusters, and check which shared water sources are involved.

Install Nerissa on your phone

Opens like an app and works offline in the field.
iPhone: how to install
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The Nerissa icon appears on your home screen.
